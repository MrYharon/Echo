Onboarding

Install Echo in 30 seconds

No account required. Operates completely in your browser with zero remote data collection.

1

Download Package

Download the unpacked extension package directly from this site and extract the zip archive to any folder on your computer.

Download echo-extension.zip ↓
2

Open Extensions Tab

In Google Chrome, Brave, or Edge, visit chrome://extensions and toggle the Developer mode switch in the upper-right corner.

3

Load Unpacked

Click Load unpacked and select the extracted Echo folder. Open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and press Alt+E.
