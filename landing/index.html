Engineering Breakdown

How does Grammarly work? And how does Echo adopt it?

Grammarly does not rely on a single slow AI call or simplistic regex. It executes a multi-tiered pipeline combining client-side tokenizers, heuristic quality graders, and deep semantic rewrite models.

Tier 1 • Instant Feedback

DOM Tokenizer & Squiggly Underlines

Grammarly tracks cursor position and contenteditable DOM changes in under 15ms. Echo does the same across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini textboxes, flagging weak starters, conversational filler, and missing constraints without keystroke lag.

< 15ms Latency • 100% Local
Tier 2 • Quality Analysis

Intent & Prompt Quality Grader

Grammarly evaluates 4 pillars: Correctness, Clarity, Engagement, and Delivery. Echo evaluates prompts across 5 AI-critical pillars: Concrete Task, Action Directives, Specific Constraints, Intent Domain, and Single-Ask Focus.

0-100 Quality Score • Heuristic Engine
Tier 3 • Deep Enhancement

Meta-Prompt Architecture & SLM

When rewriting, Grammarly passes the text and intent to a fine-tuned model. Echo transforms raw prompts into structured specifications with personas, objectives, specifications, and negative constraints using on-device Chrome Nano or cloud AI.

Chrome Nano • Gemini 1.5 • Prompt Architect
